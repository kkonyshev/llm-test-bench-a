### Problem 1:
Maria buys 7 notebooks at $4 each and 12 pens at $1.50 each.
Cost of notebooks = 7 * 4 = $28
Cost of pens = 12 * 1.50 = $18
Total cost = 28 + 18 = $46
With a 10% discount: 46 * 0.90 = $41.40

ANSWER 1: $41.40

### Problem 2:
Let Cara's current age be C.
Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: Ben = 2*(C - 6)
In 4 years, Ben will be 30: Ben + 4 = 30 => Ben = 26
So, 2*(C - 6) = 26 => C - 6 = 13 => C = 19

ANSWER 2: 19

### Problem 3:
From the clues:
- Alice does not own the fish (so Alice owns cat or dog).
- The dog owner drinks coffee.
- Ben drinks juice.
- Cleo does not drink tea (so Cleo drinks coffee or juice).

Possible assignments:
- Ben drinks juice, so Ben does not drink coffee. Thus, the dog owner must be Alice or Cleo.
- Cleo does not drink tea, so Cleo drinks coffee or juice. But Ben drinks juice, so Cleo drinks coffee.
- Thus, Cleo owns the dog (since she drinks coffee).
- Alice does not own the fish, so Alice owns the cat.
- Ben owns the fish.

ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog, Ben owns the fish

### Problem 6:
Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours, so rate = 1/6 tank per hour.

### Problem 9:
X says: "At least one of us is a liar."
- If X is a truth-teller, then at least one of X or Y is a liar. But X is a truth-teller, so Y must be a liar.
- If X is a liar, then the statement "At least one of us is a liar" is false, meaning neither X nor Y is a liar. But this contradicts X being a liar.
Thus, X must be a truth-teller, and Y is a liar.

ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller, Y is a liar
