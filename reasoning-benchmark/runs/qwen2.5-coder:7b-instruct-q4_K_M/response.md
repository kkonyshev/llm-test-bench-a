1. First, calculate the total cost before the discount.
   - Cost of notebooks: \(7 \times 4 = 28\) dollars.
   - Cost of pens: \(12 \times 1.50 = 18\) dollars.
   - Total cost before discount: \(28 + 18 = 46\) dollars.
   - Discount: \(10\% \text{ of } 46 = 0.10 \times 46 = 4.60\) dollars.
   - Total cost after discount: \(46 - 4.60 = 41.40\) dollars.
   ANSWER 1: 41.40

2. Let Cara's current age be \(C\).
   - Cara was \(C - 6\) years old 6 years ago.
   - Ben is twice Cara's age 6 years ago: \(2(C - 6)\).
   - In 4 years, Ben will be \(2(C - 6) + 4\).
   - This is given as 30 years old: \(2(C - 6) + 4 = 30\).
   - Solve for \(C\):
     \[
     2(C - 6) + 4 = 30
     \]
     \[
     2C - 12 + 4 = 30
     \]
     \[
     2C - 8 = 30
     \]
     \[
     2C = 38
     \]
     \[
     C = 19
     \]
   ANSWER 2: 19

3. (a) Alice does not own the fish.
   (b) The dog owner drinks coffee.
   (c) Ben drinks juice.
   (d) Cleo does not drink tea.
   - From (b), if the dog owner drinks coffee, then Cleo cannot drink coffee, and since Cleo does not drink tea, she must drink juice.
   - Ben drinks juice, so Ben cannot own the dog. Since Alice does not own the fish, and Ben cannot own the dog, the dog must be owned by Cleo.
   - Since Ben drinks juice, Ben cannot own the dog, and Cleo owns the dog, the fish must be owned by Alice (since Alice does not own the fish).
   - Therefore, Alice owns the fish, Ben drinks juice, and Cleo owns the dog.
   ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog, and Ben owns the fish.

4. The sequence is: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30.
   - The differences between consecutive terms are: \(6-2=4\), \(12-6=6\), \(20-12=8\), \(30-20=10\).
   - The differences form an arithmetic sequence with a common difference of 2.
   - The next difference should be \(10+2=12\).
   - The next term in the sequence is \(30+12=42\).
   ANSWER 4: 42

5. The farmer starts with 17 sheep and loses \(17 - 9 = 8\) sheep.
   ANSWER 5: 8

6. Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours, so the rate is \(\frac{1}{6}\) of the tank per hour.
   - Pipe B fills the tank in 3 hours, so the rate is \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tank per hour.
   - Combined rate: \(\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\) of the tank per hour.
   - Time to fill the tank together: \(\frac{1}{\frac{1}{2}} = 2\) hours.
   ANSWER 6: 2

7. A kilogram is approximately 2.20462 pounds.

   ANSWER 7: A kilogram of feathers

8. Let the original cost be \(C\).
   - Retail price is \(C + 0.25C = 1.25C\).
   - During the sale, the retail price is discounted by 20%: \(1.25C \times (1 - 0.20) = 1.25C \times 0.80 = 1C\).
   - The sale price is 100% of the original cost.
   ANSWER 8: 100%

9. X says: "At least one of us is a liar."
   - If X is a truth-teller, then at least one of them is a liar. This means Y must be a liar.
   - If X is a liar, then it is not true that at least one of them is a liar. This means both X and Y must be truth-tellers, which is a contradiction.
   - Therefore, X must be a liar.
   - Since X is a liar, Y must be a truth-teller.
   ANSWER 9: X is a liar, Y is a truth-teller

10. Solve the equation: \(3x + 7 = 2x + 15\).
    \[
    3x + 7 = 2x + 15
    \]
    \[
    3x - 2x = 15 - 7
    \]
    \[
    x = 8
    \]
    ANSWER 10: 8

11. The number of ways to choose 3 people from 5 candidates is given by the combination formula \(\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}\):
    \[
    \binom{5}{3} = \frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = \frac{5!}{3! \cdot 2!} = \frac{5 \times 4 \times 3!}{3! \times 2 \times 1} = \frac{5 \times 4}{2 \times 1} = 10
    \]
    ANSWER 11: 10

12. Dan is taller than Ellie, Ellie is shorter than Farah, and Farah is shorter than Dan. This implies:
    - Farah is taller than Ellie.
    - Ellie is taller than Dan.
    - Therefore, Farah is the tallest and Dan is the shortest.
    ANSWER 12: Farah is the tallest, Dan is the shortest.
