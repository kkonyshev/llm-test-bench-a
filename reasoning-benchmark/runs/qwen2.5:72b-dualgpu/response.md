1. First, calculate the total cost before the discount:
   - Cost of notebooks = 7 notebooks * $4/notebook = $28
   - Cost of pens = 12 pens * $1.50/pen = $18
   - Total cost before discount = $28 + $18 = $46
   - Discount = 10% of $46 = 0.10 * $46 = $4.60
   - Total cost after discount = $46 - $4.60 = $41.40

   ANSWER 1: 41.40

2. Let's denote Ben's current age as B and Cara's current age as C.
   - In 4 years, Ben will be 30: B + 4 = 30 → B = 26
   - Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: B = 2(C - 6)
   - Substitute B = 26 into the equation: 26 = 2(C - 6)
   - 26 = 2C - 12 → 38 = 2C → C = 19

   ANSWER 2: 19

3. We need to match each person to their pet and drink based on the clues:
   - Clue (a): Alice does not own the fish.
   - Clue (b): The dog owner drinks coffee.
   - Clue (c): Ben drinks juice.
   - Clue (d): Cleo does not drink tea.
   
   From clue (c), Ben drinks juice, so he cannot be the dog owner (clue b). Therefore, Ben cannot own the dog. Since Cleo does not drink tea (clue d), she must drink coffee or juice. But Ben already drinks juice, so Cleo must drink coffee and hence owns the dog. Alice, therefore, must drink tea and own the fish. This leaves Ben with the cat.
   
   ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog, Ben owns the cat

6. Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours, so its rate is 1/6 of the tank per hour. Pipe B fills the tank in 3 hours, so its rate is 1/3 of the tank per hour. When both are open, their combined rate is:
   - Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 of the tank per hour
   - Time to fill the tank = 1 / (1/2) = 2 hours

   ANSWER 6: 2

8. Let the original cost be C.

   - Sale price = 1.25C - 0.20 * 1.25C = 1.25C - 0.25C = 1.00C
   - Sale price as a percentage of the original cost = (1.00C / C) * 100% = 100%

   ANSWER 8: 100

9. If X is a truth-teller, then his statement "At least one of us is a liar" must be true, which means at least one of them (X or Y) is a liar. If X is a liar, then his statement "At least one of us is a liar" would be false, implying that neither of them is a liar, which is a contradiction. Therefore, X must be a truth-teller, and since at least one of them is a liar, Y must be the liar.

   ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller, Y is a liar

11. The number of different committees possible is calculated using combinations:
