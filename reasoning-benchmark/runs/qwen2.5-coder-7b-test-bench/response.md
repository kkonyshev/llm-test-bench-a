1. Maria buys 7 notebooks and 12 pens. The cost of notebooks is \(7 \times 4 = 28\) dollars, and the cost of pens is \(12 \times 1.5 = 18\) dollars. The total cost before the discount is \(28 + 18 = 46\) dollars. With a 10% discount, she pays \(46 \times 0.9 = 41.4\) dollars.
   
   ANSWER 1: 41.4

2. Let Cara's current age be \(C\). Six years ago, Cara was \(C - 6\) years old. Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago, so Ben's age is \(2(C - 6)\). In 4 years, Ben will be 30, so \(2(C - 6) + 4 = 30\). Solving for \(C\), we get \(2C - 12 + 4 = 30\), which simplifies to \(2C - 8 = 30\). Adding 8 to both sides gives \(2C = 38\), and dividing by 2 gives \(C = 19\). So, Cara is currently 19 years old.
   
   ANSWER 2: 19

3. From clue (a), Alice does not own the fish, so she owns either the cat or the dog. From clue (b), the dog owner drinks coffee, so Ben must own the dog because Alice does not. From clue (c), Ben drinks juice, but since Ben owns the dog, this clue must refer to a different person, which means there is a contradiction. Let's re-evaluate: Since Ben drinks juice and owns the dog, and Alice does not own the fish, Alice must own the cat. Therefore, Cleo must own the fish. The drink preferences must be adjusted: Ben drinks juice, Alice drinks tea, and Cleo drinks coffee. So, Ben owns the dog, and Alice owns the cat.
   
   ANSWER 3: Ben owns the dog, and Alice owns the cat.

4. The sequence is \(2, 6, 12, 20, 30\). We can see that each number is \(2\) more than the previous number multiplied by \(1\) (i.e., \(2 \times 1 + 2, 2 \times 2 + 2, 2 \times 3 + 2, 2 \times 4 + 2\)). The next number is \(2 \times 5 + 2 = 12\).
   
   ANSWER 4: 12

5. If all but 9 sheep die, the farmer has \(17 - 9 = 8\) sheep left.

6. Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours, and Pipe B fills it in 3 hours. When both pipes are open, the combined rate is \(\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\) of the tank per hour. So, it takes \(2\) hours to fill the tank.
   
   ANSWER 6: 2

If X is a liar, then it is not true that at least one of them is a liar, which means both of them must be truth-tellers. Since X is the one saying something, and the statement is false, X must be a liar. Therefore, Y is also a liar.
   
   ANSWER 9: X is a liar, and Y is a liar.
