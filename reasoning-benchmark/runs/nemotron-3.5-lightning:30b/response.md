**Problem 1:**  
Notebooks: 7 × $4 = $28  
Pens: 12 × $1.50 = $18  
Total before discount: $28 + $18 = $46  
10% discount: $46 × 0.10 = $4.60  
Amount paid: $46 – $4.60 = **$41.40**

**Problem 2:**  
Ben’s age in 4 years = 30, so Ben’s current age = 30 – 4 = 26.  
Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: 26 = 2 × (Cara’s age 6 years ago)  
Cara’s age 6 years ago = 26 ÷ 2 = 13  
Cara’s current age = 13 + 6 = **19**

**Problem 3:**  
Drinks: Ben drinks juice (clue c). Cleo does not drink tea (clue d), so Cleo drinks coffee (since juice is taken). Alice then drinks tea.  
Pets: The dog owner drinks coffee (clue b), so Cleo owns the dog. Alice does not own the fish (clue a), and the only remaining pets are cat and fish; thus Alice owns the cat, and Ben owns the fish.  
Dog owner: **Cleo**; Ben’s pet: **fish**.

**Problem 4:**  
Sequence: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ...  
Differences: +4, +6, +8, +10 (increasing by 2 each time).  
Next difference = 12, so next term = 30 + 12 = **42**.  
(Also recognizable as n(n+1) for n=1,2,3,4,5,6 → 42.)

**Problem 5:**

**9** sheep left.

**Problem 6:**  
Pipe A rate: 1/6 tank/hr; Pipe B rate: 1/3 tank/hr.  
Combined rate: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 tank/hr.  
Time to fill = 1 ÷ (1/2) = **2 hours**.

**Problem 8:**  
Let original cost = C.  
Retail price = C × 1.25.  
Sale price = Retail price × (1 – 0.20) = 1.25C × 0.80 = 1.00C.  
Sale price is **100%** of the original cost.

**Problem 9:**  
X says: "At least one of us is a liar."  
If X were a liar, the statement would be false → neither is a liar → both truth-tellers, contradicting X being a liar.  
Thus X must be a truth-teller, making the statement true → at least one liar → since X is truthful, Y is the liar.  
**X is a truth-teller, Y is a liar.**
