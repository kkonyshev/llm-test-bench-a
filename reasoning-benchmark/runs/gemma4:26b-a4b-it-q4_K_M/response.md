1. Maria buys 7 notebooks at \$4 each, which is \$28. She buys 12 pens at \$1.50 each, which is \$18. The total cost before the discount is \$28 + \$18 = \$46. A 10% discount on \$46 is \$4.60. Therefore, she pays \$46 - \$4.60 = \$41.40.

2. Let $B$ be Ben's current age and $C$ be Cara's current age. From the problem, we know that in 4 years, Ben will be 30, so $B + 4 = 30$, which means $B = 26$. We also know Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago, so $B = 2(C - 6)$. Substituting $B = 26$ into the equation gives $26 = 2(C - 6)$, so $13 = C - 6$, which means $C = 19$.

3. We have three people (Alice, Ben, Cleo), three pets (cat, dog, fish), and three drinks (tea, coffee, juice). 
   - From (c), Ben drinks juice.
   - Since Ben drinks juice, and (b) says the dog owner drinks coffee, Ben cannot own the dog.
   - The remaining drinks are tea and coffee. From (d), Cleo does not drink tea, so Cleo must drink coffee.
   - This leaves tea for Alice.
   - From (b), the dog owner drinks coffee, so Cleo owns the dog.
   - The remaining pets are cat and fish. From (a), Alice does not own the fish, so Alice must own the cat.
   - This leaves the fish for Ben.
   So, Cleo owns the dog, and Ben owns the fish.

4. The differences between consecutive numbers in the sequence are: 6 - 2 = 4; 12 - 6 = 6; 20 - 12 = 8; 30 - 20 = 10. The differences are increasing by 2 each time (4, 6, 8, 10). The next difference should be 12. Therefore, the next number is 30 + 12 = 42.

5. If all but 9 sheep die, it means 9 sheep are still alive.

6. Pipe A's rate is 1/6 of a tank per hour, and Pipe B's rate is 1/3 of a tank per hour. Their combined rate is 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 of a tank per hour. Thus, it will take 2 hours to fill the tank.

7. A kilogram is approximately 2.2 pounds. Therefore, a kilogram of feathers (2.2 lbs) is heavier than a pound of steel (1 lb).

8. Let the original cost be $C$. The retail price is $1.25C$. A 20% discount on the retail price is $1.25C \times 0.80 = 1.00C$. The sale price is 100% of the original cost.

9. If X were a liar, the statement "At least one of us is a liar" would be false, meaning both X and Y are truth-tellers. This creates a contradiction because X cannot be a truth-teller and a liar at the same time. Therefore, X must be a truth-teller. If X is a truth-teller, the statement must be true, which means at least one of them is a liar. Since X is a truth-teller, Y must be the liar.

10. Subtract 2x from both sides: $3x - 2x + 7 = 15 \Rightarrow x + 7 = 15$. Subtract 7 from both sides: $x = 8$.

11. The number of ways to choose 3 people from 5 is given by the combination formula $\binom{5}{3} = \frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = \frac{120}{6 \times 2} = 10$.
