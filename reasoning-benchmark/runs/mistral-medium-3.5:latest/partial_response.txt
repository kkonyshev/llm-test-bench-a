**2. Age Problem**
- Let Ben's current age = B, Cara's current age = C.
- In 4 years, Ben will be 30: B + 4 = 30 → B = 26.
- Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: 26 = 2 × (C - 6) → C - 6 = 13 → C = **19**.

**3. Logic Puzzle (Pets and Drinks)**
- From (c): Ben drinks juice.
- From (b): Dog owner drinks coffee → Ben does **not** own the dog (since he drinks juice).
- From (d): Cleo does not drink tea → Cleo drinks coffee or juice. But Ben drinks juice, so Cleo drinks coffee.
- From (b): Cleo drinks coffee → Cleo owns the dog.
- From (a): Alice does not own the fish → Alice owns the cat.
- Remaining: Ben must own the **fish**.
- **Answer**: Cleo owns the dog, Ben owns the fish.
