**Problem 1:**
Cost of notebooks: $7 \times \$4 = \$28$
Cost of pens: $12 \times \$1.50 = \$18$
Subtotal: $\$28 + \$18 = \$46$
Discount: $10\%$ of $\$46 = \$4.60$
Final Price: $\$46 - \$4.60 = \$41.40$
ANSWER 1: 41.4

**Problem 2:**
Let $B$ be Ben's age and $C$ be Cara's age.
"In 4 years, Ben will be 30": $B + 4 = 30 \Rightarrow B = 26$.
"Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago": $26 = 2(C - 6)$.
$13 = C - 6 \Rightarrow C = 19$.
ANSWER 2: 19

**Problem 3:**
Clues: (a) Alice $\neq$ Fish, (b) Dog $\rightarrow$ Coffee, (c) Ben $\rightarrow$ Juice, (d) Cleo $\neq$ Tea.
Since Ben drinks Juice, the remaining drinks for Alice and Cleo are Tea and Coffee.
Since Cleo $\neq$ Tea, Cleo must drink Coffee.
Since Dog owner drinks Coffee, Cleo owns the Dog.
Since Alice does not own the Fish, and Cleo owns the Dog, Ben must own the Fish.
ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog, Ben owns the fish

**Problem 4:**
Sequence differences: $6-2=4$, $12-6=6$, $20-12=8$, $30-20=10$.
The differences increase by 2 each time. The next difference is $10+2=12$.
Next number: $30 + 12 = 42$.
ANSWER 4: 42

**Problem 6:**
Pipe A rate: $1/6$ tank/hour.
Pipe B rate: $1/3$ tank/hour.
Combined rate: $1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2$ tank/hour.
Time to fill: $1 / (1/2) = 2$ hours.
ANSWER 6: 2

**Problem 8:**
Retail Price = $1.25 \times \text{Cost}$.
Sale Price = $(1 - 0.20) \times \text{Retail Price} = 0.80 \times 1.25 \times \text{Cost} = 1.00 \times \text{Cost}$.
Sale Price is 100% of original cost.
ANSWER 8: 100%

**Problem 9:**
If X is a Liar, his statement "At least one of us is a liar" is false, meaning neither is a liar (both are Truth-tellers). This contradicts the assumption that X is a Liar. Thus, X must be a Truth-teller.
Since X is a Truth-teller, his statement is true, meaning at least one is a liar. Since X is not a liar, Y must be a liar.
ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller, Y is a liar
