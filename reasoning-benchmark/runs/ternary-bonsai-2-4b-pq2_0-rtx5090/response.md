1. **Problem 1 Solution:**
Maria buys 7 notebooks at $4 each ($28) and 12 pens at $1.50 each ($18).
Total cost before discount: $28 + $18 = $46.
Discount is 10%: $46 \times 0.10 = $4.60.
Final price: $46 - $4.60 = $41.40.
ANSWER 1: 41.40

2. **Problem 2 Solution:**
Let $B$ be Ben's age and $C$ be Cara's age.
In 4 years, Ben is 30: $B + 4 = 30 \Rightarrow B = 26$.
Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: $26 = 2(C - 6)$.
$13 = C - 6 \Rightarrow C = 19$.
ANSWER 2: 19

Clues: (c) Ben drinks juice. (d) Cleo does not drink tea. (b) Dog owner drinks coffee.

Dog owner drinks coffee $\rightarrow$ Cleo owns the Dog.
Alice does not own the fish (a), so Alice owns the Cat.

4. **Problem 4 Solution:**

$2 \times 3 = 6$
$3 \times 4 = 12$
$4 \times 5 = 20$
$5 \times 6 = 30$

6. **Problem 6 Solution:**
Pipe A fills 1 tank in 6 hours ($1/6$ rate).
Pipe B fills 1 tank in 3 hours ($1/3$ rate).
Combined rate: $1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2$ tank/hour.
Time to fill 1 tank: $1 / (1/2) = 2$ hours.
ANSWER 6: 2

9. **Problem 9 Solution:**
X says "At least one of us is a liar."
If X is a truth-teller, the statement is true, so Y must be a liar.
If X is a liar, the statement is false, meaning neither is a liar (both are truth-tellers). This contradicts X being a liar.
Thus, X is a truth-teller and Y is a liar.
ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller, Y is a liar
