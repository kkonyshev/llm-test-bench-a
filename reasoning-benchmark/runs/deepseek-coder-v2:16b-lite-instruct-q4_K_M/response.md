 Let's solve each of the problems step by step.

**Problem 1:**
- Cost of 7 notebooks: \( 7 \times 4 = 28 \) dollars.
- Cost of 12 pens: \( 12 \times 1.50 = 18 \) dollars.
- Total cost before discount: \( 28 + 18 = 46 \) dollars.
- 10% discount: \( 46 \times 0.10 = 4.60 \) dollars.
- Total cost after discount: \( 46 - 4.60 = 41.40 \) dollars.

ANSWER 1: 41.40

**Problem 2:**
- Let Ben's current age be \( B \).
- Cara's age 6 years ago: \( B - 6 \).
- Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: \( B = 2 \times (B - 6) \).
- Solving for \( B \): \( B = 2B - 12 \) -> \( B = 12 \).
- In 4 years, Ben will be 12 + 4 = 16 years old.
- Cara's current age: \( 12 - 6 = 6 \) years old.

ANSWER 2: 16

**Problem 3:**
- Ben drinks juice (clue c).
- The dog owner drinks coffee (clue b).
- Alice does not own the fish (clue a).
- Cleo does not drink tea (clue d).
- Therefore, Ben owns the dog.
- Since Ben drinks juice, Alice does not drink tea (clue d), and Cleo does not drink tea, the dog owner (Ben) drinks coffee (clue b).

ANSWER 3: Ben owns the dog.

**Problem 4:**
- The sequence is \( 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, \ldots \).
- The differences between consecutive terms are \( 4, 6, 8, 10, \ldots \).
- The next term: \( 30 + 12 = 42 \).

ANSWER 4: 42

**Problem 5:**
- The farmer has 17 sheep initially.
- All but 9 die: \( 17 - 9 = 8 \).

ANSWER 5: 8

**Problem 6:**
- Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours.
- Pipe B fills the tank in 3 hours.
- Together, in one hour, they fill \( \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{2} \) of the tank.
- Therefore, it takes 2 hours to fill the tank.

ANSWER 6: 2

**Problem 7:**
- A kilogram of feathers is heavier than a pound of steel.
- This is because a kilogram is a unit of mass, while a pound is a unit of weight.

ANSWER 7: A kilogram of feathers.

**Problem 8:**
- Let the original cost be \( C \).
- The retail price after a 25% markup: \( C \times 1.25 \).
- The sale price after a 20% discount: \( C \times 1.25 \times 0.80 = C \times 1.00 = C \).
- The sale price is equal to the original cost.

ANSWER 8: 100%

**Problem 9:**
- X says: "At least one of us is a liar."
- If X is a truth-teller, then X is telling the truth.
- If X is a liar, then X is lying.
- If X is a liar, then Y must be a truth-teller (since a liar cannot say something true).
- If X is a truth-teller, then Y could be either a truth-teller or a liar.
- Therefore, X could be either a truth-teller or a liar.
- Y must be a truth-teller.

ANSWER 9: X could be either a truth-teller or a liar. Y is a truth-teller.

**Problem 10:**
- \( 3x + 7 = 2x + 15 \).
- Subtract \( 2x \) from both sides: \( x + 7 = 15 \).
- Subtract 7 from both sides: \( x = 8 \).

ANSWER 10: 8

**Problem 11:**
- The number of ways to choose 3 people from 5 is given by the combination formula \( \binom{5}{3} = \frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = 10 \).

ANSWER 11: 10

**Problem 12:**
- Dan is taller than Ellie.
- Ellie is shorter than Farah.
- Farah is shorter than Dan.
- Therefore, Dan is the tallest, and Ellie is the shortest.

ANSWER 12: Dan is the tallest, Ellie is the shortest.
