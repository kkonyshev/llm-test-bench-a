1. First, calculate the cost of notebooks: 7 × $4 = $28.
   Then, calculate the cost of pens: 12 × $1.50 = $18.
   Total before discount: $28 + $18 = $46.
   Discount: 10% of $46 = $4.60.
   Final cost: $46 − $4.60 = $41.40.
   ANSWER 1: 41.40

2. In 4 years, Ben will be 30, so Ben is currently 26.
   Let Cara’s current age be C.
   Six years ago, Cara was (C − 6).
   Ben is twice that age: 26 = 2(C − 6).
   Solving: 26 = 2C − 12 → 38 = 2C → C = 19.
   Cara is 19 years old now.
   ANSWER 2: 19

3. Let's work through clues systematically:
   - Clue (c): Ben drinks juice.
   - Clue (d): Cleo does not drink tea → Cleo drinks either coffee or juice.
   - But Ben drinks juice → Cleo doesn't drink juice → Cleo drinks coffee.
   - Clue (b): Dog owner drinks coffee → Cleo owns the dog.
   - Clue (a): Alice does not own fish.
   So, Alice owns either cat or dog.
   Since Cleo owns dog → Alice owns cat.
   That leaves Ben with the fish.
   So, Cleo owns the dog, and Ben owns the fish.
   ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog; Ben owns the fish.

6. Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours → rate = 1/6 per hour.
   Pipe B fills it in 3 hours → rate = 1/3 per hour.
   Combined rate: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 per hour.
   Time to fill: 1 ÷ (1/2) = 2 hours.
   ANSWER 6: 2
