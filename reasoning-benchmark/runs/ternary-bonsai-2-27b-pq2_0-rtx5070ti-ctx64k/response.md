2. Ben will be 30 in 4 years, so Ben is now:
   \[
   30-4=26
   \]
   Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago, so Cara was 6 years ago:
   \[
   26/2=13
   \]
   Therefore, Cara is now:
   \[
   13+6=19
   \]

   \]
   of the tank per hour, so it takes:
   \[
   2
   \]
