### Problem 1:
Maria buys 7 notebooks at $4 each and 12 pens at $1.50 each. The total cost before discount is:
\[ 7 \times 4 + 12 \times 1.50 = 28 + 18 = 46 \text{ dollars} \]
With a 10% discount, the amount she pays is:
\[ 46 \times 0.90 = 41.4 \text{ dollars} \]

ANSWER 1: 41.4

### Problem 2:
Let Cara's current age be \( C \). Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago, so Ben's current age is \( 2(C - 6) \). In 4 years, Ben will be 30, so:
\[ 2(C - 6) + 4 = 30 \]
\[ 2C - 12 + 4 = 30 \]
\[ 2C - 8 = 30 \]
\[ 2C = 38 \]
\[ C = 19 \]

ANSWER 2: 19

### Problem 3:
Let's deduce the information step by step:
- From clue (a): Alice does not own the fish, so Alice owns either the cat or the dog.
- From clue (b): The dog owner drinks coffee.
- From clue (c): Ben drinks juice.
- From clue (d): Cleo does not drink tea, so Cleo drinks either coffee or juice. But Ben drinks juice, so Cleo drinks coffee.
- Since Cleo drinks coffee, and the dog owner drinks coffee, Cleo owns the dog.
- Alice does not own the fish (from clue a), so Alice owns the cat.
- Ben owns the fish.

ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog, and Ben owns the fish.

### Problem 4:
The sequence is: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ...
The pattern is \( n(n+1) \), where \( n \) starts at 1:
\[ 1 \times 2 = 2 \]
\[ 2 \times 3 = 6 \]
\[ 3 \times 4 = 12 \]
\[ 4 \times 5 = 20 \]
\[ 5 \times 6 = 30 \]
The next term is \( 6 \times 7 = 42 \).

ANSWER 4: 42

ANSWER 5: 9

### Problem 6:
Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours, so its rate is \( \frac{1}{6} \) of the tank per hour. Pipe B fills the tank in 3 hours, so its rate is \( \frac{1}{3} \) of the tank per hour. Together, their rate is:
\[ \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \text{ of the tank per hour} \]
So, it takes 2 hours to fill the tank.

ANSWER 6: 2

### Problem 8:
Let the original cost be \( C \). The retail price is \( C + 0.25C = 1.25C \). During the sale, the retail price is discounted by 20%, so the sale price is:
\[ 1.25C \times 0.80 = 1.00C \]
Thus, the sale price is 100% of the original cost.

ANSWER 8: 100

### Problem 9:
X says: "At least one of us is a liar."
- If X is a truth-teller, then the statement is true, meaning at least one of X or Y is a liar. Since X is a truth-teller, Y must be the liar.
- If X is a liar, then the statement is false, meaning neither X nor Y is a liar. But this contradicts the assumption that X is a liar. Therefore, X must be a truth-teller, and Y must be the liar.

ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller, and Y is a liar.
