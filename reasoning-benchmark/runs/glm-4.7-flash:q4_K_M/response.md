Here are the solutions to the 12 problems:

1.  **Cost of 7 notebooks:** $7 \times \$4 = \$28$.
    **Cost of 12 pens:** $12 \times \$1.50 = \$18$.
    **Total before discount:** $\$28 + \$18 = \$46$.
    **Discount:** $10\%$ of $\$46 = \$4.60$.
    **Amount paid:** $\$46 - \$4.60 = \$41.40$.

2.  Let $B$ be Ben's current age and $C$ be Cara's current age.
    Ben's statement: $B = 2 \times (C - 6)$.
    From Ben's future age: $B + 4 = 30$, so $B = 26$.
    Substitute $B$: $26 = 2 \times (C - 6)$.
    $13 = C - 6$, so $C = 19$.

3.  Let's determine the drinks first based on the clues:

    *   Clue (d): Cleo does not drink tea, so Cleo drinks coffee (since Ben drinks juice).
    *   Therefore, Alice drinks tea.
    *   Clue (b): The dog owner drinks coffee. Since Cleo drinks coffee, Cleo owns the dog.
    *   Clue (a): Alice does not own the fish. Since Cleo owns the dog, the remaining pets are Cat and Fish. Alice does not have the fish, so Alice has the cat. This leaves the fish for Ben.

4.  Look at the differences between the numbers:
    $6 - 2 = 4$
    $12 - 6 = 6$
    $20 - 12 = 8$
    $30 - 20 = 10$
    The pattern is adding even numbers increasing by 2 ($4, 6, 8, 10, \dots$).
    The next difference is 12.
    $30 + 12 = 42$.

5.  The phrase "All but 9 die" implies that everything dies except for 9 items. Therefore, the farmer has 9 sheep left.

6.  Pipe A's rate is $\frac{1}{6}$ of the tank per hour.
    Pipe B's rate is $\frac{1}{3}$ of the tank per hour (or $\frac{2}{6}$).
    Together, the rate is $\frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$ of the tank per hour.
    It takes $1 \div \frac{1}{2} = 2$ hours.

7.  A kilogram is a standard unit of mass, while a pound is an imperial unit. 1 kilogram is approximately 2.2 pounds. Therefore, a kilogram is heavier than a pound.

8.  The retail price is $100\% + 25\% = 125\%$ of the cost (or $1.25C$).
    The sale price is $125\% \times (100\% - 20\%) = 125\% \times 80\%$.
    $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1.0$.
    The sale price is exactly $100\%$ of the original cost.

9.  If **X** were a liar, his statement "At least one of us is a liar" would be false. If it were false, it would mean neither is a liar (both are truth-tellers). This contradicts the assumption that X is a liar. Therefore, **X** must be a truth-teller. Since his statement is true, **Y** is a liar.

10. $3x + 7 = 2x + 15$.
    Subtract $2x$ from both sides: $x + 7 = 15$.
    Subtract $7$ from both sides: $x = 8$.

11. This is a combination problem ($5 \text{ choose } 3$).
    $\frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = \frac{5 \times 4 \times 3!}{3! \times 2 \times 1} = \frac{20}{2} = 10$.
