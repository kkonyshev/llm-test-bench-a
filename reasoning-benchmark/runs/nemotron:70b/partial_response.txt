**1. A shop sells notebooks for $4 each and pens for $1.50 each. Maria buys 7 notebooks and 12 pens, then gets a 10% discount on the total. How much does she pay, in dollars?**

* Cost of 7 notebooks = 7 * $4 = $28
* Cost of 12 pens = 12 * $1.50 = $18
* Total cost before discount = $28 + $18 = $46
* Discount = 10% of $46 = 0.10 * $46 = $4.60
* Total cost after discount = $46 - $4.60 = $41.40

ANSWER 1: $41.40

**2. Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago. In 4 years, Ben will be 30. How old is Cara now?**

* Ben's age in 4 years = 30
* Ben's current age = 30 - 4 = 26
* Cara's age 6 years ago = Ben's current age / 2 = 26 / 2 = 13
* Cara's current age = 13 + 6 = 19

ANSWER 2: 19

**3. Three friends — Alice, Ben, and Cleo — each own exactly one pet (cat, dog, or fish) and each prefer one drink (tea, coffee, or juice).**
**Clues: (a) Alice does not own the fish. (b) The dog owner drinks coffee. (c) Ben drinks juice. (d) Cleo does not drink tea.**
**Who owns the dog, and what pet does Ben own?**

* From (c), Ben drinks juice. From (b), the dog owner drinks coffee, so Ben doesn't own the dog.
* From (b), the dog owner drinks coffee. Since Ben drinks juice, and (d) implies Cleo doesn't drink tea but could drink coffee, and Alice is left to potentially drink tea, Cleo is the most likely to own the dog based on the elimination of other drink preferences for the others.
* Given Ben doesn't own the fish (since if he did, Alice would have to own the fish to not own it, which contradicts (a)), and doesn't own the dog, Ben must own the cat.

ANSWER 3: **Dog Owner: Cleo, Ben's Pet: Cat**

**4. What is the next number in the sequence: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?**

* Sequence analysis: 
	+ 2 to 6: +4
	+ 6 to 12: +6
	+ 12 to 20: +8
	+ 20 to 30: +10
* Pattern: Increase by consecutive even numbers
* Next difference should be +12
* Next number in sequence = 30 + 12 = 42

ANSWER 4: 42

**5. A farmer has 17 sheep. All but 9 die. How many sheep does the farmer have left?**

* "All but 9 die" means 9 sheep survive
* No calculation needed based on the phrasing of the question

ANSWER 5: 9

**6. Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours. Pipe B fills the same tank in 3 hours. If both pipes are opened together, how many hours does it take to fill the tank?**

* Rate of Pipe A = 1 tank / 6 hours
* Rate of Pipe B = 1 tank / 3 hours
* Combined rate = (1/6 + 1/3) tanks per hour = (1/6 + 2/6) = 3/6 = 1/2 tank per hour
* Time to fill the tank together = 1 tank / (1/2 tank per hour) = 2 hours

ANSWER 6: 2

**7. Which is heavier: a kilogram of feathers, or a pound of steel?**

* 1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds (approximately)
* Comparison:
	+ 1 kilogram of feathers ≈ 2.20462 pounds of feathers
	+ 1 pound of steel = 1 pound
* A kilogram of feathers is heavier than a pound of steel

ANSWER 7: **A kilogram of feathers**

**8. A store marks up the cost of an item by 25% to set the retail price. During a sale, the retail price is discounted by 20%. What is the sale price as a percentage of the original cost?**

* Original cost = 100% (let's say $100 for ease)
* Markup by 25% = $100 * 1.25 = $125 (retail price)
* Discount by 20% on $125 = $125 * 0.80 = $100
* Sale price as a percentage of the original cost = ($100 / $100) * 100% = 100%

ANSWER 8: 100%

**9. On an island, every person always tells the truth or always lies. You meet two islanders, X and Y. X says: "At least one of us is a liar." Is X a truth-teller or a liar? What about Y?**

* If X is a truth-teller, then at least one of them must indeed be a liar, which means Y could be either, but this doesn't provide a direct contradiction or confirmation.
* If X is a liar, then the statement "at least one of us is a liar" must be false, meaning both X and Y are truth-tellers, which is a contradiction since X is assumed to be a liar in this scenario.
* Thus, X must be a truth-teller for the statement to hold without contradiction. Since X's statement is true, and at least one must be a liar, Y must be the liar.

ANSWER 9: **X: Truth-teller, Y: Liar**

**10. If 3x + 7 = 2x + 15, what is x?**

* Subtract 2x from both sides: 3x - 2x + 7 = 2x - 2x + 15
* Simplify: x + 7 = 15
* Subtract 7 from both sides: x = 8

ANSWER 10: 8

**11. A committee of 3 people is chosen from 5 candidates. How many different committees are possible?**

* Combination formula since order doesn't matter: C(n, k) = n! / [k!(n-k)!]
* Here, n = 5 (total candidates), k = 3 (committee size)
* C(5, 3) = 5! / [3!(5-3)!] = (5*4*3) / (3*2*1) = 10

ANSWER 11: 10

**12. Dan is taller than Ellie. Ellie is shorter than Farah. Farah is shorter than Dan. Who is the tallest, and who is the shortest?**

* Dan is taller than Ellie
* Ellie is shorter than Farah implies Farah is taller than Ellie
* Farah is shorter than Dan implies Dan is taller than Farah
* Combined: Dan > Farah > Ellie (tallest to shortest)

ANSWER 12: **Tallest: Dan, Shortest: Ellie**
