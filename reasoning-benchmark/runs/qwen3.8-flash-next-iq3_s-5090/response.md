1. Maria buys:  
   - Notebooks: 7 × $4 = $28  
   - Pens: 12 × $1.50 = $18  
   Total before discount: $28 + $18 = $46  
   10% discount means she pays 90% of $46: $46 × 0.90 = $41.40  

2. In 4 years, Ben will be 30, so Ben is currently 26.  
   Let Cara’s current age be C. Six years ago, Cara was C − 6.  
   Ben is twice that age:  
   26 = 2(C − 6)  
   26 = 2C − 12  
   38 = 2C  
   C = 19  

3. Ben drinks juice.

   Cleo does not drink tea, and Ben already drinks juice, so Cleo must drink coffee.  
   Since the dog owner drinks coffee, Cleo owns the dog.  
   Alice does not own the fish, and Cleo owns the dog, so Alice owns the cat.

4. The sequence is 2, 6, 12, 20, 30.  
   Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12.  
   30 + 12 = 42.  

5. “All but 9 die” means 9 sheep remain.  

6. Pipe A fills at 1/6 tank per hour.  
   Pipe B fills at 1/3 tank per hour.  
   Together: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 tank per hour.  
   So they fill the tank in 2 hours.  

7. A kilogram is heavier than a pound because 1 kg ≈ 2.2046 pounds.

8. Let the original cost be C.  
   Retail price after 25% markup: 1.25C  
   Sale price after 20% discount: 0.80 × 1.25C = 1.00C  
   So the sale price is 100% of the original cost.  

9. If X were a liar, then X’s statement “At least one of us is a liar” would be false, meaning neither X nor Y is a liar. But that contradicts X being a liar.  
   So X must be a truth-teller.  
   Then the statement is true: at least one of them is a liar. Since X is truthful, Y must be the liar.
