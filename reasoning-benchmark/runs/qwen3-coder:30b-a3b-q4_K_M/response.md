**Problem 1:**  
Maria buys 7 notebooks at $4 each and 12 pens at $1.50 each.  
Cost of notebooks = 7 × $4 = $28  
Cost of pens = 12 × $1.50 = $18  
Total cost before discount = $28 + $18 = $46  
Discount = 10% of $46 = 0.10 × $46 = $4.60  
Amount paid after discount = $46 - $4.60 = $41.40  

ANSWER 1: 41.40

**Problem 2:**  
In 4 years, Ben will be 30 ⇒ Ben is currently 26 years old.  
Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago ⇒ Let Cara’s current age = C  
Then, 26 = 2 × (C - 6)  
26 = 2C - 12  
38 = 2C  
C = 19  

ANSWER 2: 19

**Problem 3:**  
We are given:
- Alice does not own the fish.
- Dog owner drinks coffee.
- Ben drinks juice.
- Cleo does not drink tea.

Since Ben drinks juice, he is not the one who drinks coffee ⇒ Ben doesn't own the dog.  
The dog owner drinks coffee ⇒ so the dog owner must be either Alice or Cleo.  
Cleo does not drink tea ⇒ she can only drink juice or coffee.  
But Ben drinks juice ⇒ Cleo doesn’t drink juice ⇒ Cleo drinks coffee ⇒ Cleo owns the dog.  
Since Alice does not own the fish, and Cleo owns the dog, Alice must own the cat.  
So Ben owns the fish.

Thus:
- Dog owner: Cleo
- Ben's pet: fish

ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog; Ben owns the fish.

**Problem 4:**  
Sequence: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?  
Let’s look at differences:
- 6 - 2 = 4
- 12 - 6 = 6
- 20 - 12 = 8
- 30 - 20 = 10

Differences increase by 2 each time: 4, 6, 8, 10 ⇒ Next difference = 12  
So next term = 30 + 12 = 42  

ANSWER 4: 42

**Problem 6:**  
Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours ⇒ rate = 1/6 per hour  
Pipe B fills the tank in 3 hours ⇒ rate = 1/3 per hour  
Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 per hour  
Time to fill = 1 ÷ (1/2) = 2 hours  

ANSWER 6: 2

**Problem 8:**  
Let original cost = $100  
Retail price = 100 + 25% = $125  
Sale discount = 20% off $125 = 0.20 × $125 = $25  
Final sale price = $125 - $25 = $100  
So, sale price = original cost ⇒ 100%

ANSWER 8: 100%

**Problem 9:**  
X says: “At least one of us is a liar.”  
If X is telling the truth ⇒ then at least one of them (X or Y) is a liar. Since X speaks truth, Y must be the liar.  
If X were lying ⇒ then it's false that "at least one of us is a liar." That would mean both are truth-tellers. But then X couldn't lie — contradiction.

So X must be a truth-teller and Y must be a liar.

ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller; Y is a liar.

Choose 3 people from 5 ⇒ Use combination formula
