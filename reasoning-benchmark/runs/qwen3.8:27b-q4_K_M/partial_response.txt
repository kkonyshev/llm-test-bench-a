# Solving All 12 Problems

## Problem 1
- Notebooks: 7 × $4 = $28
- Pens: 12 × $1.50 = $18
- Subtotal: $28 + $18 = $46
- After 10% discount: $46 × 0.90 = **$41.40**

## Problem 2
- In 4 years Ben will be 30, so Ben is currently **26**.
- Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: 26 = 2(Cara − 6)
- Cara − 6 = 13 → Cara = **19**

## Problem 3
- Ben drinks juice (clue c) → Ben is NOT the dog owner (clue b: dog owner drinks coffee).
- Cleo doesn't drink tea (clue d), and juice is taken by Ben → Cleo drinks **coffee**.
- Dog owner drinks coffee (clue b) → **Cleo owns the dog**.
- Alice doesn't own the fish (clue a); remaining pets are cat and fish for Alice and Ben → Alice owns the cat, **Ben owns the fish**.

## Problem 4
2 = 1×2, 6 = 2×3, 12 = 3×4, 20 = 4×5, 30 = 5×6 → next is 6×7 = **42**

## Problem 5
"All but 9 die" means 9 survive. Answer: **9**

## Problem 6
Combined rate: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 tank per hour → Time = **2 hours**

## Problem 7
1 kilogram ≈ 2.20 pounds > 1 pound. A kilogram of feathers is heavier. Answer: **a kilogram of feathers**

## Problem 8
Sale price = Cost × 1.25 × 0.80 = Cost × 1.00 → **100%** of original cost.

## Problem 9
- If X is a liar, the statement is false → both are truth-tellers → contradiction (X can't be both).
- So X is a **truth-teller**. The statement is true → at least one is a liar → Y is the **liar**.

## Problem 10
3x + 7 = 2x + 15 → x = 15 − 7 → **x = 8**

## Problem 11
C(5,3) = 5!/(3!·2!) = (5·4)/(2·1) = **10**

Order: Dan > Farah > Ellie. Tallest: **Dan**; Shortest: **Ellie**.
