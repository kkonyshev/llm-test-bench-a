**Problem 1:**
A shop sells notebooks for $4 each and pens for $1.50 each. Maria buys 7 notebooks and 12 pens, then gets a 10% discount on the total. How much does she pay, in dollars?

- Cost of notebooks: 7 × $4 = $28
- Cost of pens: 12 × $1.50 = $18
- Total before discount: $28 + $18 = $46
- Discount: 10% of $46 = $4.60
- Final amount paid: $46 - $4.60 = $41.40

**Problem 2:**
Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago. In 4 years, Ben will be 30. How old is Cara now?

- Let Ben's current age be B, Cara's current age be C.
- In 4 years, Ben will be 30: B + 4 = 30 → B = 26.
- Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago: B = 2 × (C - 6).
- Substitute B = 26: 26 = 2 × (C - 6) → 13 = C - 6 → C = 19.

**Problem 3:**
Three friends — Alice, Ben, and Cleo — each own exactly one pet (cat, dog, or fish) and each prefer one drink (tea, coffee, or juice).
Clues: (a) Alice does not own the fish. (b) The dog owner drinks coffee. (c) Ben drinks juice. (d) Cleo does not drink tea.
Who owns the dog, and what pet does Ben own?

- From (c), Ben drinks juice.
- From (b), the dog owner drinks coffee, so Ben does not own the dog (he drinks juice).
- From (d), Cleo does not drink tea, so Cleo drinks coffee or juice. But Ben drinks juice, so Cleo drinks coffee.
- From (b), the dog owner drinks coffee, so Cleo owns the dog.
- From (a), Alice does not own the fish, so Alice owns the cat (since Cleo owns the dog).
- Ben cannot own the dog or cat (Alice owns the cat), so Ben owns the fish.

**Problem 4:**
What is the next number in the sequence: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

- The sequence is generated by n(n+1), where n starts at 1:
  1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, so the next term is 6×7=42.

**Problem 5:**
A farmer has 17 sheep. All but 9 die. How many sheep does the farmer have left?

- "All but 9 die" means 9 survive. So the farmer has 9 sheep left.

**Problem 6:**
Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours. Pipe B fills the same tank in 3 hours. If both pipes are opened together, how many hours does it take to fill the tank?

- Pipe A's rate: 1/6 tank per hour.
- Pipe B's rate: 1/3 tank per hour.
- Combined rate: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/6 + 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 tank per hour.
- Time to fill the tank: 1 / (1/2) = 2 hours.

**Problem 7:**
Which is heavier: a kilogram of feathers, or a pound of steel?

- 1 kilogram of feathers weighs 1000 grams.
- 1 pound of steel weighs approximately 453.59 grams.
- So, a kilogram of feathers is heavier.

**Problem 8:**
A store marks up the cost of an item by 25% to set the retail price. During a sale, the retail price is discounted by 20%. What is the sale price as a percentage of the original cost?

- Let the original cost be C.
- Retail price: C + 25% of C = 1.25C.
- Sale price: 1.25C - 20% of 1.25C = 1.25C × 0.80 = 1.00C.
- So, the sale price is 100% of the original cost.

**Problem 9:**
On an island, every person always tells the truth or always lies. You meet two islanders, X and Y. X says: "At least one of us is a liar." Is X a truth-teller or a liar? What about Y?

- If X is a truth-teller, then his statement "At least one of us is a liar" must be true. This means at least one of X or Y is a liar. But X is a truth-teller, so Y must be a liar.
- If X is a liar, then his statement "At least one of us is a liar" is false. This means neither X nor Y is a liar, which contradicts the assumption that X is a liar.
- Therefore, X must be a truth-teller, and Y must be a liar.

**Problem 10:**
If 3x + 7 = 2x + 15, what is x?

- Subtract 2x from both sides: x + 7 = 15.
- Subtract 7 from both sides: x = 8.

**Problem 11:**
A committee of 3 people is chosen from 5 candidates. How many different committees are possible?

- This is a combination problem: C(5, 3) = 5! / (3! × (5-3)!) = (5 × 4) / (2 × 1) = 10.

**Problem 12:**
Dan is taller than Ellie. Ellie is shorter than Farah. Farah is shorter than Dan. Who is the tallest, and who is the shortest?

- From the statements:
  - Dan > Ellie
  - Farah > Ellie
  - Dan > Farah
- Order: Dan > Farah > Ellie.
- So, Dan is the tallest, and Ellie is the shortest.
