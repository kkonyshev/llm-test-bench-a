**Problem 1**  
Maria buys 7 notebooks at $4 each → $28, and 12 pens at $1.50 each → $18. Total = $46. 10% discount = $4.60. Amount paid = $46 - $4.60 = $41.40.

**Problem 2**  
Let Cara’s age = \(C\). Ben’s age = \(B\).  
\(B = 2(C - 6)\). In 4 years, \(B + 4 = 30\) → \(B = 26\).  
Then \(26 = 2(C - 6)\) → \(C - 6 = 13\) → \(C = 19\).

**Problem 3**  
- Ben drinks juice (clue c).  
- Cleo does not drink tea (d) → Cleo drinks coffee (since juice is taken).  
- Dog owner drinks coffee (b) → Cleo owns the dog.  
- Alice does not own fish (a) → Alice owns cat (dog is taken).  
Thus Ben owns the fish.  

**Problem 4**  
Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10 → next difference 12 → 30 + 12 = 42.  
(Or pattern: \(1\times2, 2\times3, 3\times4, 4\times5, 5\times6\) → next \(6\times7 = 42\).)

**Problem 5**  
“All but 9 die” means all except 9 die, so 9 survive.

**Problem 6**  
Pipe A rate = \(1/6\) tank per hour. Pipe B rate = \(1/3\) tank per hour. Combined rate = \(1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2\) tank per hour → time = \(1 / (1/2) = 2\) hours.

**Problem 7**  
1 kilogram = approximately 2.2 pounds, so a kilogram is heavier. A kilogram of feathers is heavier than a pound of steel.

**Problem 8**  
Retail price = \(1.25\) × cost. 20% discount → sale price = \(1.25 \times 0.8 = 1.0\) × cost → 100% of original cost.

**Problem 9**  
If X were a liar, his statement “At least one of us is a liar” would be false, meaning both are truth-tellers, contradicting X being a liar. Thus X is a truth-teller. Since the statement is true, at least one is a liar → Y is the liar.

**Problem 10**  
\(3x + 7 = 2x + 15\) → \(x = 8\).

**Problem 11**  
Number of committees from 5 candidates choose 3: \(\binom{5}{3} = 10\).

**Problem 12**  
Dan > Ellie, Farah > Ellie, Farah < Dan → Dan > Farah > Ellie. Tallest: Dan, shortest: Ellie.
