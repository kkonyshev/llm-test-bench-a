1. **Notebook and Pen Purchase:**
   - Cost of notebooks: \(7 \times 4 = 28\) dollars
   - Cost of pens: \(12 \times 1.50 = 18\) dollars
   - Total cost before discount: \(28 + 18 = 46\) dollars
   - Discount: \(10\% \times 46 = 4.60\) dollars
   - Total cost after discount: \(46 - 4.60 = 41.40\) dollars

   ANSWER 1: 41.40

2. **Ben and Cara's Ages:**
   - In 4 years, Ben will be 30, so Ben is currently \(30 - 4 = 26\) years old.
   - Ben is twice as old as Cara was 6 years ago, so \(26 = 2 \times (Cara's age - 6)\)
   - \(26 = 2 \times (Cara's age - 6)\)
   - \(13 = Cara's age - 6\)
   - Cara's age = \(13 + 6 = 19\)

   ANSWER 2: 19

3. **Pet and Drink Ownership:**
   - From clue (b), the dog owner drinks coffee.
   - From clue (c), Ben drinks juice.
   - From clue (d), Cleo does not drink tea, so Cleo drinks coffee.
   - Since Cleo drinks coffee, Cleo must own the dog.
   - Alice does not own the fish (clue a), so Alice must own the cat.
   - Ben, by process of elimination, must own the fish.

   ANSWER 3: Cleo owns the dog, Ben owns the fish

4. **Sequence:**
   - The sequence is: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
   - Each term can be expressed as \(n(n + 1)\) where \(n\) is the position in the sequence.
   - For the 6th term: \(6 \times 7 = 42\)

   ANSWER 4: 42

5. **Sheep Survivors:**
   - The farmer has 17 sheep, and all but 9 die.
   - Therefore, the farmer has 9 sheep left.

   ANSWER 5: 9

6. **Tank Filling:**
   - Pipe A fills the tank in 6 hours, so its rate is \(\frac{1}{6}\) of the tank per hour.
   - Pipe B fills the tank in 3 hours, so its rate is \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tank per hour.
   - Combined rate: \(\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\) of the tank per hour.
   - Time to fill the tank: \(\frac{1}{\frac{1}{2}} = 2\) hours

   ANSWER 6: 2

7. **Weight Comparison:**

8. **Markup and Discount:**
   - Let the original cost be \(C\).
   - Retail price after 25% markup: \(1.25C\)
   - Sale price after 20% discount: \(0.80 \times 1.25C = 1.00C\)
   - The sale price is 100% of the original cost.

   ANSWER 8: 100%

9. **Islanders' Truth:**
   - If X is a truth-teller, then "At least one of us is a liar" is true, which is consistent.
   - If X is a liar, then "At least one of us is a liar" is false, which means neither X nor Y is a liar, which is a contradiction.
   - Therefore, X is a truth-teller, and Y is a liar.

   ANSWER 9: X is a truth-teller, Y is a liar

10. **Equation Solution:**
    - \(3x + 7 = 2x + 15\)
    - Subtract \(2x\) from both sides: \(x + 7 = 15\)
    - Subtract 7 from both sides: \(x = 8\)

    ANSWER 10: 8

11. **Committee Selection:**
    - The number of ways to choose 3 people from 5 is given by the combination formula \( \binom{5}{3} \).
    - \(\binom{5}{3} = \frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = \frac{5 \times 4}{2 \times 1} = 10\)

    ANSWER 11: 10
